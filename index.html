Personal projects · Three.js

3D product configurators for e-commerce

Two configurators I built to show production-style 3D commerce work: real-time colour, material, part, finish and size switching; an optimised GLB pipeline; hotspots and animated camera views; and add-to-cart through the Shopify Cart API. By Haseeb Ahmed Danish, senior full-stack developer.

NFC business card

PVC, metal or wood. Live text and logo, printed or laser-engraved with real surface depth, front/back flip, and pack pricing mirroring a live Shopify catalogue.

  • Canvas textures
  • Bump engraving
  • PBR materials
  • Shopify variants

Lounge chair

Swap legs (tapered, hairpin, swivel), arms and pillow; bouclé, velvet or leather; wood and metal finishes; three sizes with live dimensions.

  • GLB + meshopt
  • Part variants
  • Hotspots
  • Size morphing

Fast by design

Renders only when something changes, so an idle page uses no GPU. The chair is 8 draw calls, and its model is 72 KB after meshopt compression (747 KB raw).

Data-driven models

Parts, variants, material slots and hotspots come from glTF extras, so a re-exported model works without code changes.

Shopify-ready

Each configuration maps to a variant plus line-item properties, posted to /cart/add.js. A Liquid section mounts it in any theme.
